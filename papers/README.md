# Downloaded Papers

PDFs were downloaded to this directory and chunked into `papers/pages/` with the provided PDF chunker at 3 pages per chunk. All PDFs were validated with `pypdf`.

1. [Bongard-LOGO: A New Benchmark for Human-Level Concept Learning and Reasoning](2010.00763_bongard_logo_benchmark.pdf)
   - Authors: Weili Nie, Zhiding Yu, Lei Mao, Ankit B. Patel, Yuke Zhu, Animashree Anandkumar
   - Year: 2020/2021
   - Source: arXiv 2010.00763, NeurIPS 2020
   - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.00763
   - Why relevant: Core synthetic 2D geometry benchmark with repeated positive/negative examples and ground-truth LOGO action programs.

2. [Visual Spatial Reasoning](2205.00363_visual_spatial_reasoning.pdf)
   - Authors: Fangyu Liu, Guy Emerson, Nigel Collier
   - Year: 2022/2023
   - Source: arXiv 2205.00363, TACL 2023
   - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.00363
   - Why relevant: Controlled image-text spatial-relation dataset useful for addressing and relation-verbalization baselines.

3. [PuzzleVQA: Diagnosing Multimodal Reasoning Challenges of Language Models with Abstract Visual Patterns](2403.13315_puzzlevqa_abstract_patterns.pdf)
   - Authors: Yew Ken Chia, Vernon Toh Yan Han, Deepanway Ghosal, Lidong Bing, Soujanya Poria
   - Year: 2024
   - Source: arXiv 2403.13315, Findings ACL 2024
   - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13315
   - Why relevant: Abstract pattern VQA with ground-truth perception, induction, and deduction explanations.

4. [Reasoning Limitations of Multimodal Large Language Models: A Case Study of Bongard Problems](2411.01173_bongard_reasoning_limitations_mllms.pdf)
   - Authors: Mikolaj Malkinski, Szymon Pawlonka, Jacek Mandziuk
   - Year: 2024/2025
   - Source: arXiv 2411.01173, ICML 2025
   - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.01173
   - Why relevant: Directly evaluates MLLMs on Bongard problems with direct, descriptive, iterative, and contrastive prompting strategies.

5. [Forgotten Polygons: Multimodal Large Language Models are Shape-Blind](2502.15969_forgotten_polygons_shape_blind.pdf)
   - Authors: William Rudman, Michal Golovanevsky, Amir Bar, Vedant Palit, Yann LeCun, Carsten Eickhoff, Ritambhara Singh
   - Year: 2025
   - Source: arXiv 2502.15969, Findings ACL 2025
   - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15969
   - Why relevant: Controlled evidence that MLLMs fail at simple polygon identification and side-counting, especially for novel shapes.

6. [VisuLogic: A Benchmark for Evaluating Visual Reasoning in Multi-modal Large Language Models](2504.15279_visulogic_visual_reasoning.pdf)
   - Authors: Weiye Xu et al.
   - Year: 2025
   - Source: arXiv 2504.15279
   - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.15279
   - Why relevant: Visual-centric reasoning benchmark designed to reduce text-only shortcuts, including spatial and attribute reasoning.

7. [Spatial-DISE: A Unified Benchmark for Evaluating Spatial Reasoning in Vision-Language Models](2510.13394_spatial_dise_benchmark.pdf)
   - Authors: Xinmiao Huang et al.
   - Year: 2025/2026
   - Source: arXiv 2510.13394, ICLR 2026
   - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13394
   - Why relevant: Taxonomy and dataset for intrinsic/extrinsic, static/dynamic spatial reasoning; useful for evaluation taxonomy.

8. [Symbolic Grounding Reveals Representational Bottlenecks in Abstract Visual Reasoning](2604.21346_symbolic_grounding_bottlenecks.pdf)
   - Authors: Mohit Vaishnav, Tanel Tammet
   - Year: 2026
   - Source: arXiv 2604.21346
   - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21346
   - Why relevant: Most directly aligned with the hypothesis; compares raw-image VLM performance with LLM reasoning over symbolic LOGO-style descriptions.
